# CoalHearth Privacy Policy

**CoalHearth collects nothing and phones nowhere.**

- **No telemetry.** No usage data, analytics, or identifiers are collected, stored, or transmitted — by any hook or bundled component.
- **No network calls from the hooks.** Every hook is offline by design (Phoenix Commandment #7): they read local files, journal locally, and print only on their sanctioned channel. No sockets, no requests. (The self-update *check* is the agent's `/coalhearth:update` procedure, run only with your consent — never a hook.)
- **It runs inside YOUR agent.** CoalHearth operates no servers and receives no traffic. It calls no model API; everything happens in your Claude Code session, on your account, under your platform's permission gate.
- **The journal is a snapshot of YOUR session, kept on YOUR disk.** It records the goal, checklist, planned steps, modified-file *names* (read best-effort from the `Write`/`Edit`/etc. tool-call payloads the hook observes — no `git` is run), the *path* to your own Claude Code session transcript (used only to detect whether it's been garbage-collected, never its contents), and — for a subagent tool call (`Agent`/`Task`/`Workflow`) that has resolved — a short, size-capped (300 char) status/outcome snippet read best-effort from that subagent's own tool-response (issue #13; a `PostToolUse`-observed field, not a separate read of the subagent's transcript). Everything here stays on your disk, never uploaded anywhere.
- **Error reports are manual.** When something misbehaves, your agent may *offer* to open a pre-filled GitHub issue; nothing is submitted automatically, and you see and edit the full contents first.
- **Local files only.** All state lives in files you can read: the journal `<project>/.claude/coalhearth/session_handoff.json` (a corrupt journal is quarantined aside as `session_handoff.corrupt.json` in the same directory; it also carries a `transcriptPath` field pointing at your own local CC transcript file, never its contents), a `.gitignore` (just `*`) in that same directory so its contents never end up in your project's version control even if your own `.gitignore` doesn't cover `.claude/`, the config (`~/.claude/.coalhearth.json` global, plus an optional per-project `coalhearth.json` — read order: your own agent's dir first, then `.claude` → `.agents` → `.gemini`, then the legacy root `.coalhearth.json`; see README's Configure section), and the self-update throttle stamp `~/.claude/coal/coalhearth/update-check` (was `~/.claude/.coalhearth-update-check` before 2026-08-08 — both are read, the old one is dropped on the next scheduled write; a timestamp, nothing more).

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/TheColliery/CoalHearth/issues>.
